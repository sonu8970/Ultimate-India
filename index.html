Chai Run

Your chai is running away, and you are chasing it through the street. Tap left or right to change lanes, dodge cows, autos and cycles, and grab every cup of hot chai to push your score higher.

Free to play. Quick to learn, hard to put down.

Download for Android

Android APK. If your phone asks, allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser, then open the file to install.
